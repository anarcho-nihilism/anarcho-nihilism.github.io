# Manifesto

Political left and right were left behind somewhere at the beginning of the 20th century. Even authoritarian and non-authoritarian political standards have deflated, showing the failure of their own utopian projects.

So what can we talk and write about on these pages? What is our main weapon?

The naked truth. Truth stripped bare like a live wire, sparking with the horror of philistine hopes and the collapse of the idealists' faith.

Faith in better worlds shatters against the reality of what *Homo sapiens* is. And the struggle against the system, stretched over decades, makes it obvious how hollow it is to dream of freedom in the past or to wait for it in the future.

Liberation finds itself in manifestation. Here and now.

Only in action aimed at the pillars of oppression does that precious second still remain, when you can forget your own chains and the chains of others.

The moment of horror in the eyes of the filth standing in front of you on the square. The fear of the philistine who hears your uncompromising call through the megaphone. The flicker of police lights in the fog as the car races after you and your comrades, past the Chaos you have made in the streets. The sweaty forehead of a billionaire giving comments after yet another case like Luigi Mangione's.

The stinking carcass of the system has created conditions that it cannot bear itself. Falling apart in the convulsions of wars, genocides, and mass and individual terror, it cannot escape one of the main laws of the universe: decay.

We are particles of entropy. We only continue and bring closer the inevitable: the total and unconditional collapse of all institutions and mechanisms of the existing order.

Freedom in the moment…

Freedom in manifestation…

Freedom here and now, and nowhere else!
